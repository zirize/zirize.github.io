Privacy Policy

vadroid · Last updated 2 November 2026

vadroid sends nothing to the developer. It has no server of its own, no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. It talks to exactly one place: a PC that you own and pair the app with, reached over your home network or your own VPN.

What the app is

vadroid is a personal voice assistant. You speak to your phone; the phone turns your speech into text; that text goes to an assistant program (the "relay") running on your own PC; the relay's reply comes back as text, and the phone reads it aloud.

How your voice and text flow

  • Microphone. The app uses the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) only while you are talking to it. It does not record audio to a file and does not send audio anywhere itself.
  • Speech recognition. Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service (Android's SpeechRecognizer; on most phones this is provided by Google). That service is part of your device, not of this app, and handles audio under its provider's own privacy policy.
  • Text to your PC. Only the recognized text is sent, to the relay on your PC. The app accepts only addresses on a home network or a VPN range, and only the PC you paired it with.
  • What your PC does with it. The relay on your PC sends that text to AI services you have set up under your own accounts — currently the Google Gemini API and Anthropic Claude — to produce the reply. Those services process the text under their own terms and privacy policies. The developer of this app receives none of it.
  • Replies and notifications. Text replies and notification messages come back from your PC to the phone, are shown as notifications and read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech engine.

Background connection

When you turn it on, the app keeps its connection to your PC open with a foreground service, so that replies and messages from your PC can arrive while the screen is off. A persistent notification is shown the whole time, and you can stop it from that notification or from the app.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps the connection details for your PC (its address and the login made for this app when you paired it) in Android's private per-app storage on your device. Nothing of this leaves your device by any action of the app, except that it is used to log in to your own PC.

If you have Android device backup turned on, this private storage may be included in your own Google account backup. That is a function of Android and your Google account; the developer has no access to it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone. Anything kept on your PC, or by the AI services your PC uses, is under your control through that PC and those accounts.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
